Tandoori Chicken Wraps

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes

Quick and easy chicken wraps with tandoori-spiced chicken, fresh salad and mayonnaise. Perfect for a busy weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 3 chicken breasts, diced
  • 2 tbsp tandoori seasoning
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 iceberg lettuce, shredded
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • Mayonnaise
  • 8 wraps

Instructions

  1. Toss the diced chicken in the tandoori seasoning until evenly coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the chicken until fully cooked through and lightly charred.
  4. Prepare the lettuce, tomato and cucumber.
  5. Warm the wraps if desired.
  6. Spread mayonnaise onto each wrap.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato and cucumber.
  8. Top with the tandoori chicken.
  9. Roll up the wraps and serve.

Notes

  • One of the quickest meals in the recipe library.
  • Works well with leftover cooked chicken.
  • Kids often prefer the salad served separately.
  • Can be served with crisps, chips or potato wedges.
