Roast Leg of Lamb Dinner

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 25 minutes
Cook Time: 1 hour 45 minutes

A proper roast leg of lamb dinner with gravy, roast potatoes, parsnips, broccoli, carrots and buttery sweet potato mash.

Ingredients

Lamb

  • 1.8-2kg leg of lamb
  • 2 tbsp light olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • Fine sea salt
  • Black pepper

Trivet and Gravy

  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 carrot, roughly chopped
  • 1 stick celery or piece of celeriac, roughly chopped
  • 1 bay leaf
  • A few black peppercorns
  • 500ml lamb, beef or chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cornflour, mixed with a little cold water (optional)

Trimmings

  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and cut for roasting
  • 4 parsnips, peeled and cut for roasting
  • 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • Butter
  • Broccoli
  • Carrots

Instructions

  1. Remove the lamb from its packaging, pat dry and bring towards room temperature for 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan or 215°C without a fan.
  3. Make small cuts in the lamb and tuck in the sliced garlic and rosemary leaves.
  4. Rub the lamb with olive oil, then season well with fine sea salt and black pepper.
  5. Make a trivet in a deep roasting tray with the onion, carrot, celery or celeriac, bay leaf, rosemary stalks and black peppercorns.
  6. Place the lamb on top of the trivet.
  7. Roast in the centre of the oven for 20 minutes.
  8. Reduce the oven temperature to 180°C fan and continue roasting for 1 hour 10 minutes to 1 hour 25 minutes, depending on how well done you like the lamb.
  9. For medium lamb, aim for a core temperature of about 60-65°C before resting. For well done, cook to about 70°C.
  10. Remove the lamb from the oven, transfer to a clean tray, cover loosely with foil and rest for at least 20 minutes before carving.
  11. Leave the roasted vegetables in the tray for the gravy.
  12. While the lamb cooks, prepare the roast potatoes and parsnips. Roast them together until golden and crisp.
  13. Steam the sweet potatoes until tender, then mash with butter.
  14. Steam or boil the broccoli and carrots until tender.
  15. To make the gravy, deglaze the roasting tray with 500ml lamb, beef or chicken stock, stirring up all the caramelised juices.
  16. Pass the gravy through a fine sieve into a clean saucepan, pushing the juices from the roasted vegetables through.
  17. Bring the gravy to a simmer and thicken if needed with cornflour mixed with cold water. Reduce until rich and glossy.
  18. Carve the lamb and serve with roast potatoes, parsnips, sweet potato mash, broccoli, carrots and gravy.

Notes

  • This uses the same roast dinner trimmings as the roast chicken recipe.
  • For a 1.8-2kg leg of lamb, expect around 1 hour 30-45 minutes roasting plus resting.
  • Resting the lamb makes it easier to carve and keeps it juicier.
  • Roast potatoes and parsnips can be cooked together to keep things simple.
  • Mint sauce or redcurrant jelly work well on the side.
