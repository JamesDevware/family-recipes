Homemade Pizza

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 12 minutes

A simple homemade pizza method using pizza dough, passata, mozzarella and cheddar. Add family favourites like pepperoni, peppers, ham or sweetcorn.

Ingredients

  • 500g pizza dough
  • 150ml passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 mozzarella ball, drained and torn
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • Plain flour, for dusting
  • Pepperoni, peppers, ham or sweetcorn, for topping
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 240C fan, or as hot as it will go. Put baking trays in the oven to heat up.
  2. Mix the passata with oregano, olive oil, a pinch of salt and black pepper.
  3. Divide the pizza dough into 2 or 4 pieces and roll out on a floured surface.
  4. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over each base, leaving a small border around the edge.
  5. Top with mozzarella, cheddar and toppings such as pepperoni, peppers, ham or sweetcorn.
  6. Carefully transfer to the hot trays and bake for 10-12 minutes until crisp and bubbling.
  7. Cool for 2 minutes before slicing and serving.

Notes

  • Shop-bought pizza dough is fine for a quick dinner.
  • Keep toppings fairly light so the base cooks properly.
  • Let children choose their own toppings on smaller individual pizzas.
