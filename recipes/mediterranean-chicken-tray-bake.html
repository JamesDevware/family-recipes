Mediterranean Chicken Tray Bake

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes

A simple one-tray chicken dinner with peppers, courgette, cherry tomatoes, onion and dried thyme.

Ingredients

  • 1kg chicken thighs
  • 2 peppers, sliced
  • 1 courgette, sliced
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 250g cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
  2. Add the peppers, courgette, red onion and cherry tomatoes to a large roasting tray.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with dried thyme, salt and black pepper.
  4. Toss the vegetables until evenly coated.
  5. Nestle the chicken thighs among the vegetables.
  6. Season the chicken with a little extra salt, black pepper and dried thyme.
  7. Roast for 40-45 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are soft and lightly caramelised.
  8. Serve with rice, couscous, flatbreads or crusty bread.

Notes

  • Good for using up peppers, courgettes, onions and tomatoes.
  • Works well with rice, couscous, flatbreads or crusty bread.
  • One tray, minimal washing up.
