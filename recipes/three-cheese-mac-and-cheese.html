Three Cheese Mac and Cheese

A rich family-style mac and cheese made with cheddar, mozzarella and Italian hard cheese, finished in the oven with a crisp cheesy top.

Ingredients

  • 400g macaroni or short pasta
  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 600ml milk
  • 150g mature cheddar, grated
  • 1 mozzarella ball, drained and torn
  • 50g Italian hard cheese, grated, plus extra for topping
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
  2. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until just under al dente, then drain.
  3. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes to make a roux.
  4. Gradually whisk in the milk, a little at a time, until smooth. Simmer for 3–5 minutes until thickened.
  5. Turn the heat low and stir in the cheddar, mozzarella, Italian hard cheese and Dijon mustard if using. Season with salt and black pepper.
  6. Add the cooked pasta to the cheese sauce and mix until fully coated.
  7. Transfer to an ovenproof dish, grate extra Italian hard cheese over the top and bake for 15–20 minutes until bubbling and crisp on top.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Notes

  • Add cooked bacon, broccoli or peas if you want to bulk it out.
  • For a looser sauce, add a splash more milk before baking.
  • Best served fresh, but leftovers reheat well with a splash of milk.
