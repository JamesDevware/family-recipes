Homemade Cheeseburgers and Chips

Simple homemade cheeseburgers served with crispy air fryer chips made from fresh potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Burger toppings of choice (lettuce, tomato, onion, burger sauce)

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into chip-sized pieces.
  2. Place in cold water, bring to the boil and cook for 15 minutes.
  3. Drain and allow to steam dry.
  4. Toss with oil, salt and pepper.
  5. Air fry at 200°C for 25–30 minutes, turning occasionally.
  6. Form the beef mince into four burger patties and season.
  7. Cook until done and add cheese for the final minute.
  8. Toast the buns if desired.
  9. Assemble burgers with toppings and serve with the chips.

Notes

  • The chip method works well for wedges too.
  • Any burger toppings can be added depending on what's in the fridge.
  • Kids often prefer plain burgers with ketchup and chips.
