Rich Tomato Beef Pasta

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes

A simple family pasta dish with beef mince, tomato sauce and Italian herbs. One of those reliable weeknight meals that everyone will eat.

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 500g pasta
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 beef stock pot
  • 1–2 tbsp tomato puree
  • 400g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Italian mixed herbs
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, fry the onion until softened.
  3. Add the beef mince and cook until browned.
  4. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Add the tomato puree and cook for another minute.
  6. Add the Worcestershire sauce, beef stock pot, chopped tomatoes and Italian herbs.
  7. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until thickened.
  8. Season to taste.
  9. Drain the pasta and mix through the sauce before serving.

Notes

  • Leftovers reheat well the next day.
  • Can be frozen in portions.
  • Serve with grated hard cheese if desired.
