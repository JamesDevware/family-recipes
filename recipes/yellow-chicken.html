Yellow Chicken

Serves: 6
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 40 minutes

Named by my daughter. This is her favourite meal: a simple one-pot chicken, rice and peas dinner with turmeric, cumin and paprika.

Ingredients

  • 2kg chicken thighs
  • 400g basmati rice
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 1 litre chicken stock
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken thighs with salt, black pepper, paprika, cumin and turmeric.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
  3. Sear the chicken thighs on both sides until golden brown, then remove them from the pot and set aside.
  4. In the same pot, add the chopped onions and sauté until soft and translucent, about 5–7 minutes.
  5. Add the minced garlic and ginger, then cook for another 2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Pour in the chicken stock and bring it to a boil.
  7. Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium-low, add the chicken back in, cover the pot and cook for 10 minutes.
  8. Add the basmati rice to the pot with the chicken and stir thoroughly. Cover again and cook for a further 10 minutes.
  9. Stir in the peas, bring back to the boil and continue cooking for 5–10 minutes, until the chicken and rice are fully cooked and the liquid is absorbed.
  10. Remove the pot from the heat and let it sit, covered, for 5 minutes.
  11. Serve the chicken, rice and peas hot.

Notes

  • Kid-friendly and good for batch cooking.
  • Works well with boneless or bone-in chicken thighs, but cooking time may vary.
  • Leftovers reheat well for lunch the next day.
