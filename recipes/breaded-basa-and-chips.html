Breaded Basa and Chips

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes

Homemade breaded basa fillets served with air fryer chips, peas and tartare sauce. A cheap and surprisingly good fish and chips night.

Ingredients

  • 4 basa fillets
  • 4 large potatoes
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300g frozen peas
  • Tartare sauce
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into chip-sized pieces.
  2. Place the chips into a saucepan of cold water and bring to the boil. Cook for 15 minutes.
  3. Drain and allow to steam dry.
  4. Toss with oil, salt and pepper.
  5. Air fry at 200°C for 25–30 minutes, turning occasionally.
  6. Dip the basa fillets into beaten egg, then coat thoroughly in breadcrumbs.
  7. Air fry the breaded basa at 200°C until cooked through and golden.
  8. Cook the peas.
  9. Serve with tartare sauce.

Notes

  • The same chip method is used for burgers and chips.
  • Basa is inexpensive but works really well when breaded at home.
  • Kids tend to prefer these over shop-bought fish fingers.
