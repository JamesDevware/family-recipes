Chicken Fajita Rice Bowls

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes

Chicken fajita rice bowls with tender chicken thighs, peppers, onion and warm fajita seasoning. Serve with sour cream, lime and fresh coriander.

Ingredients

  • 600g boneless skinless chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 300g long grain rice
  • 600ml chicken stock or water
  • 120g sour cream, to serve
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Fresh coriander, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice in chicken stock or water according to the packet instructions, then keep warm.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the chicken thighs and cook for 6-8 minutes until browned and nearly cooked through.
  4. Add the peppers and onion, then cook for another 6-8 minutes until softened.
  5. Sprinkle over the fajita seasoning with a splash of water and stir until coated and cooked through.
  6. Spoon the rice into bowls and top with the chicken fajita mixture.
  7. Serve with sour cream, lime wedges and fresh coriander.

Notes

  • Use a mild fajita seasoning for younger children.
  • Add sweetcorn, avocado or grated cheese if you want extra toppings.
  • The chicken mixture also works well in wraps the next day.
