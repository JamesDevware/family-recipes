Chicken Kebab Tray Bake

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes

A practical chicken kebab tray bake made with chicken mince, peppers and red onion. Serve in warm flatbreads with salad and a simple Greek yogurt sauce.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken mince
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • Small handful fresh coriander, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 peppers, sliced
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 4 flatbreads
  • 120g Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salad leaves, cucumber and tomato, to serve

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200C fan and line a large baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Mix the chicken mince with the garlic, paprika, cumin, salt, black pepper and most of the coriander.
  3. Shape the mixture into 8 small kebab-style logs and place them on the tray.
  4. Add the peppers and red onion around the kebabs, drizzle with olive oil and toss lightly.
  5. Bake for 22-25 minutes, turning the kebabs halfway through, until cooked through.
  6. Mix the Greek yogurt with lemon juice and the remaining coriander.
  7. Warm the flatbreads and serve with the kebabs, roasted vegetables, salad and yogurt sauce.

Notes

  • Use mild paprika for a family-friendly version, or smoked paprika for a deeper flavour.
  • If the chicken mixture feels sticky, lightly oil your hands before shaping the kebabs.
  • Leftover kebabs are good cold in lunchbox wraps the next day.
