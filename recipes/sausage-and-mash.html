Sausage and Mash

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes

A simple family favourite with sausages, mashed potatoes, peas and onion gravy.

Ingredients

  • 12 sausages
  • 1 brown onion, sliced
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp Bisto gravy granules
  • Water
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel and chop the potatoes and place them into a pan of salted water.
  2. Boil until tender, then drain.
  3. Mash with butter, milk, salt and black pepper.
  4. Meanwhile, fry the sausages and onion until the sausages are browned and the onion has softened.
  5. Add water and stir in the Bisto gravy granules.
  6. Bring to a gentle boil.
  7. Add the peas, cover and cook until the sausages are fully cooked and the peas are tender.
  8. Serve the sausages, peas and onion gravy over mashed potatoes.

Notes

  • A great midweek comfort food dinner.
  • Any good quality pork sausages work well.
  • Extra gravy is never a bad idea.
