Sausage Pasta Bake

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes

A straightforward sausage pasta bake with a tomato sauce, mozzarella and cheddar. Bake until golden and bubbling for an easy family dinner.

Ingredients

  • 6 sausages
  • 350g pasta shapes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 500g passata
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 mozzarella ball, drained and torn
  • 100g cheddar, grated
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200C fan.
  2. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until just under al dente, then drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan.
  4. Squeeze the sausage meat from the skins into the pan and cook until browned.
  5. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Stir in the passata and oregano, season, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Mix the pasta into the sauce and transfer to an ovenproof dish.
  8. Top with mozzarella and cheddar, then bake for 15-20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Notes

  • Use pork, chicken or veggie sausages depending on what you have.
  • Add spinach, peas or sweetcorn to the sauce for extra vegetables.
  • Leftovers reheat well for lunch the next day.
