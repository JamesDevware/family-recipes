Roast Pork Shoulder Dinner

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 25 minutes
Cook Time: 2 hours 40 minutes

A proper roast pork shoulder dinner with gravy, roast potatoes, parsnips, broccoli, carrots and buttery sweet potato mash.

Ingredients

Pork

  • 2kg pork shoulder joint
  • Goose fat, duck fat or light olive oil
  • Fine sea salt

Trivet and Gravy

  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 carrot, roughly chopped
  • 1 stick celery or piece of celeriac, roughly chopped
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 sprig thyme
  • A few black peppercorns
  • 1 Bramley apple, peeled and quartered (optional)
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cornflour, mixed with a little cold water (optional)

Trimmings

  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and cut for roasting
  • 4 parsnips, peeled and cut for roasting
  • 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • Butter
  • Broccoli
  • Carrots

Instructions

  1. Remove the pork shoulder from its packaging, pat dry and bring to room temperature.
  2. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan or 215°C without a fan.
  3. Season the pork just before roasting. If the joint does not have a generous coating of fat, massage with a little goose fat, duck fat or light olive oil, then season well with fine sea salt.
  4. Make a trivet in a deep roasting tray with the onion, carrot, celery or celeriac, bay leaf, thyme, black peppercorns and optional Bramley apple.
  5. Place the pork skin-side up on top of the trivet.
  6. Roast in the centre of the oven for 20 minutes.
  7. Reduce the oven temperature to 190°C and continue roasting for 30 minutes per 500g, until the pork reaches a minimum core temperature of 70°C.
  8. For a 2kg pork shoulder, this means approximately 2 hours 20 minutes total roasting time.
  9. Remove the pork from the oven, transfer to a clean tray, cover loosely with foil and rest for at least 20 minutes before carving.
  10. Leave the roasted vegetables in the tray for the gravy.
  11. While the pork cooks, prepare the roast potatoes and parsnips. Roast them together until golden and crisp.
  12. Steam the sweet potatoes until tender, then mash with butter.
  13. Steam or boil the broccoli and carrots until tender.
  14. To make the gravy, deglaze the roasting tray with 500ml chicken stock, stirring up all the caramelised juices.
  15. Pass the gravy through a fine sieve into a clean saucepan, pushing the juices from the roasted vegetables through.
  16. Bring the gravy to a simmer and thicken if needed with cornflour mixed with cold water. Reduce until rich and glossy.
  17. Carve the pork and serve with roast potatoes, parsnips, sweet potato mash, broccoli, carrots and gravy.

Notes

  • This is a hotter, shorter pork shoulder roast rather than a low-and-slow pulled pork method.
  • Use 30 minutes per 500g after the initial 20-minute blast.
  • For a 2kg pork shoulder, expect around 2 hours 20 minutes roasting plus resting.
  • Roast potatoes and parsnips can be cooked together to keep things simple.
  • The sweet potatoes are steamed and mashed with butter.
