Pathia Style Chicken Curry

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 1 hour

A sweet, tangy and slightly spicy chicken curry inspired by a Pathia. The vinegar and sugar are adjusted at the end to get the balance just right.

Ingredients

  • 1kg chicken thighs, on the bone
  • 2 brown onions, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp medium curry powder
  • 1 tbsp tomato puree
  • 400g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Chilli powder, to taste
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Cook the onions slowly until deeply golden.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the curry powder and tomato puree and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and stir well.
  5. Add the chicken thighs and coat in the sauce.
  6. Simmer gently until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  7. Add the vinegar and sugar.
  8. Adjust the balance until you get the sweet and tangy flavour you like.
  9. Add chilli powder to taste.
  10. Season and serve with rice.

Notes

  • The onions should be properly golden before adding the spices.
  • The vinegar and sugar are adjusted by taste rather than exact measurements.
  • Even better the next day.
  • Works well for batch cooking.
