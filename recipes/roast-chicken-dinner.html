Roast Chicken Dinner

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 25 minutes
Cook Time: 1 hour 45 minutes

A proper roast chicken dinner with gravy, roast potatoes, parsnips, broccoli, carrots and buttery sweet potato mash.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1 large or extra-large whole chicken, about 1.8-2.2kg
  • Goose fat, duck fat, butter or light olive oil
  • Fine sea salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved

Trivet and Gravy

  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 carrot, roughly chopped
  • 1 stick celery or piece of celeriac, roughly chopped
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 sprig thyme
  • A few black peppercorns
  • 500ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cornflour, mixed with a little cold water (optional)

Trimmings

  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and cut for roasting
  • 4 parsnips, peeled and cut for roasting
  • 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • Butter
  • Broccoli
  • Carrots

Instructions

  1. Remove the chicken from its packaging, pat dry and bring towards room temperature for 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan or 215°C without a fan.
  3. Season the chicken just before roasting. Rub the skin with goose fat, duck fat, butter or light olive oil, then season well with fine sea salt and black pepper.
  4. Place the lemon halves inside the chicken cavity.
  5. Make a trivet in a deep roasting tray with the onion, carrot, celery or celeriac, bay leaf, thyme and black peppercorns.
  6. Place the chicken breast-side up on top of the trivet.
  7. Roast in the centre of the oven for 20 minutes.
  8. Reduce the oven temperature to 180°C fan and continue roasting for about 20 minutes per 500g, until the chicken reaches a minimum core temperature of 75°C and the juices run clear.
  9. For a 2kg chicken, this means approximately 1 hour 40 minutes total roasting time.
  10. Remove the chicken from the oven, transfer to a clean tray, cover loosely with foil and rest for at least 20 minutes before carving.
  11. Leave the roasted vegetables in the tray for the gravy.
  12. While the chicken cooks, prepare the roast potatoes and parsnips. Roast them together until golden and crisp.
  13. Steam the sweet potatoes until tender, then mash with butter.
  14. Steam or boil the broccoli and carrots until tender.
  15. To make the gravy, deglaze the roasting tray with 500ml chicken stock, stirring up all the caramelised juices.
  16. Pass the gravy through a fine sieve into a clean saucepan, pushing the juices from the roasted vegetables through.
  17. Bring the gravy to a simmer and thicken if needed with cornflour mixed with cold water. Reduce until rich and glossy.
  18. Carve the chicken and serve with roast potatoes, parsnips, sweet potato mash, broccoli, carrots and gravy.

Notes

  • This is a classic roast chicken method for a large or extra-large bird.
  • Use about 20 minutes per 500g after the initial 20-minute blast.
  • For a 2kg chicken, expect around 1 hour 40 minutes roasting plus resting.
  • The chicken is done when the thickest part reaches 75°C and the juices run clear.
  • Roast potatoes and parsnips can be cooked together to keep things simple.
