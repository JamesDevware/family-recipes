Pork Satay Tray Bake

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes

A family-friendly tray bake combining tender pork loin, roasted vegetables and a rich satay-inspired peanut sauce. Perfect served with rice and ideal for leftovers.

Ingredients

  • 800g pork loin joint, cut into bite-sized chunks
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 2 red peppers, sliced
  • 1 yellow pepper, sliced
  • 1 large red onion, cut into wedges
  • 1 courgette, sliced (optional)
  • 4 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3–4 tbsp water
  • Cooked rice, to serve
  • Fresh coriander, chopped
  • Lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan.
  2. Mix the pork with the oil, paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper.
  3. Add the vegetables to a large roasting tray and scatter the pork over them.
  4. Whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, honey, sweet chilli sauce, lime juice, garlic and water to create the satay sauce.
  5. Pour two-thirds of the sauce over the pork and vegetables and mix gently.
  6. Roast for 25–30 minutes, stirring halfway through cooking.
  7. Drizzle with the remaining sauce, sprinkle with coriander and serve with rice and lime wedges.

Notes

  • For a milder family version, omit the sweet chilli sauce.
  • Add broccoli, baby corn or mangetout for extra vegetables.
  • Leftovers work brilliantly in wraps the next day.
