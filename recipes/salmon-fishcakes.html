Salmon Fishcakes

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 25 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes

Simple salmon fishcakes made with flaked salmon, mashed potato, spring onions and breadcrumbs. Serve with peas, salad or chips.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 700g potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 4 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 100g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Peas, salad or chips, to serve

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15-18 minutes until tender, then drain and mash.
  2. Bake or microwave the salmon fillets until just cooked through, then remove the skin and flake the fish.
  3. Mix the mashed potato with the flaked salmon, spring onions, lemon juice, salt and black pepper.
  4. Shape the mixture into 8 fishcakes.
  5. Dust lightly with flour, dip in beaten egg and coat with breadcrumbs.
  6. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the fishcakes for 4-5 minutes on each side until golden and hot through.
  7. Serve with peas, salad or chips.

Notes

  • Chill the shaped fishcakes for 20 minutes before frying if you have time; it helps them hold together.
  • Panko breadcrumbs give the crispest coating, but standard breadcrumbs work well.
  • Cooked leftover salmon can be used instead of cooking fillets from scratch.
