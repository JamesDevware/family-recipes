Chilli Con Carne

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 40 minutes

A mild family-friendly chilli con carne with beef mince, kidney beans and a rich tomato sauce. Serve with rice for an easy weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp mild chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp tomato puree
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp brown sugar (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 300g long grain rice, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and mild chilli powder, then cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the beef mince and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon.
  5. Stir in the tomato puree, chopped tomatoes, kidney beans, beef stock and brown sugar if using.
  6. Bring to a simmer and cook gently for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  7. Season with salt and black pepper, then serve with cooked rice.

Notes

  • This is deliberately mild; add more chilli powder at the table for anyone who wants heat.
  • Leftovers freeze well. Cool fully, portion into tubs and freeze for up to 3 months.
  • Serve with grated cheddar, sour cream or tortilla chips if you like.
