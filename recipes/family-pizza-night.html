Family Pizza Night

Serves: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes

Homemade pizza night with fresh dough, mozzarella and BBQ chicken wings. A regular family favourite.

Ingredients

Pizza Dough

  • 500g strong white bread flour
  • 7g fast-action dried yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 325ml warm water

Pizza

  • 200ml passata
  • 2 mozzarella balls, drained and torn
  • Pizza toppings of choice such as pepperoni, ham, peppers or sweetcorn

BBQ Wings

  • 1kg chicken wings
  • BBQ sauce, enough to coat the wings

Instructions

  1. Add the flour, yeast, salt, olive oil and warm water to the bowl of a stand mixer.
  2. Fit the dough hook and mix for around 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover and leave to rise for about 1 hour, or until doubled in size.
  4. Divide into two dough balls and stretch into pizza bases.
  5. Spread passata over the bases and add torn mozzarella and toppings of choice.
  6. Bake at 220°C fan for 10–15 minutes until the base is cooked and the cheese is bubbling.
  7. Coat the chicken wings generously in BBQ sauce.
  8. Air fry the wings at 200°C for approximately 25 minutes, turning halfway through, until cooked through and lightly charred.
  9. Serve the pizzas alongside the BBQ wings.

Notes

  • Kids can choose and add their own toppings.
  • The dough can be made earlier in the day and left to rise slowly.
  • Extra dough freezes well.
  • Friday night favourite.
